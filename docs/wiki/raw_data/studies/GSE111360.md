## Study summary
- **Goal**: Establish a 3D organoid culture preserving native tumor immune cells and stromal components for studying immune checkpoint blockade.

- **Findings**: Demonstrated organoid platform retains tumor-immune interactions and recapitulates checkpoint blockade response.
