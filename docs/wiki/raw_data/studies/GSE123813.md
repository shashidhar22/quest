## Study summary
- **Goal**: Determine whether checkpoint blockade reinvigorates pre-existing tumor-specific T cells or recruits new clones.
- **Method**: Paired scRNA + scTCR-seq from BCC and SCC patient TILs pre/post anti-PD-1.
- **Findings**: Successful response involves expansion of novel clones rather than reinvigoration of pre-existing tumor-reactive cells (clonal replacement).
