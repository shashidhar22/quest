## Study summary
- **Goal**: Characterize TCR Vbeta oligoclonal expansion and CDR3 homology in expanded CD8+CD57+ effector memory T cells in acquired aplastic anemia.
- **Method**: Flow cytometry sorting plus deep TCR-Vbeta sequencing of CD8+CD57+ subsets from AA patients and matched controls.
- **Findings**: Found frequent TCR Vbeta oligoclonality and CDR3 homology indicating antigen-driven expansion in AA.

## Caveats / notes
- Bulk TCR survey only (TRB chain); no epitope or MHC labels.
- Limited cohort size; clinical correlates from a single-center study.
