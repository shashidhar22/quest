## Study summary
- **Goal**: Demonstrate TIRTL-seq, an affordable method for deep paired TCR repertoire sequencing.
- **Method**: 4 TIRTL-seq experiments, 1 10x scTCR, 1 5'RACE bulk; CD4/CD8 enrichment plus antigen stimulation series.
- **Findings**: TIRTL-seq matches commercial methods at lower cost; reveals SARS-CoV-2-specific clonal dynamics.
